I am a **Research Scientist at Amazon Robotics**, where I develop optimization and AI-driven methods for decision-making in large-scale operational systems. My research lies at the intersection of **operations research, mathematical optimization, machine learning, and large-scale cyber-physical systems**.

I received my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering (Transportation Systems) from **North Carolina State University** in 2021. My doctoral research focused on network-level traffic signal control in connected and automated transportation systems. Before joining industry, I conducted research at **Oak Ridge National Laboratory** and **Washington State University**.

## Research interests

- Large-scale mathematical and combinatorial optimization
- AI-accelerated optimization and learning-to-optimize
- Distributed optimization and multi-agent coordination
- Agentic AI for adaptive decomposition of optimization problems
- Intelligent transportation and infrastructure systems
- Data-driven decision-making for robotics and automation

## Current research direction

A central challenge in large-scale optimization is producing high-quality decisions within the time limits of real operational systems. I am interested in combining optimization with learning-based and agentic AI methods that can identify problem structure, decompose complex decisions, coordinate interacting subproblems, and adapt their strategies using solver feedback.

My broader goal is to develop generalizable computational methods that bridge rigorous optimization models with the speed and adaptability required by real-world infrastructure and automation systems.
